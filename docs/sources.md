## FATF documents

1. **Guidance for a Risk-Based Approach: The Banking Sector**, October 2014. Official landing page: https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/publications/fatfrecommendations/documents/risk-based-approach-banking-sector.html . Explicit warning from FATF: this guidance does not incorporate post-publication revisions, including changes to Recommendation 1 in 2025.
2. **Trade-Based Money Laundering: Risk Indicators**, 11 March 2021. Official page: https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/publications/Methodsandtrends/Trade-based-money-laundering-indicators.html . Indicators support monitoring and investigation; one indicator does not establish money laundering.
3. **Operational Issues — Financial Investigations Guidance**, 2012. Official page: https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/publications/Methodsandtrends/Operationalissues-financialinvestigationsguidance.html . Describes operational investigation methods; not a substitute for current local law.
